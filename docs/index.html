how lumen works

1 the drive

a place in Finder for the files that outgrow your Mac.

mount Lumen next to Macintosh HD. the footage in your bucket shows up as files and folders your editor already knows how to open.

on Windows, open the mount in File Explorer. on Linux, use it like any other filesystem path.

2 what happens when you open a file

skip to the middle of a film and lumen fetches the chunks around that frame. it decrypts them on your machine and keeps them in a cache for repeat reads.

the rest of the film can stay in the bucket. there’s no whole-file download to wait for.

  • 3operating systems
  • 1 MiBtarget chunk size
  • 256-bitAES chunk keys

3 getting a file onto lumen

  1. install lumen with macFUSE on Mac, WinFsp on Windows or FUSE on Linux.
  2. mount the drive with your bucket and local index.
  3. drag a file into the volume. open it from that same path.

4 inside the drive

  • a normal mount for your desktop apps
  • a cache that can read ahead as you play
  • FastCDC uploads that reuse unchanged chunks
  • your choice of S3-compatible storage
  • MCP tools for your agents
  • files encrypted before upload
  • separate counts for bytes stored and retrieved

why i built Lumen.

Notes

i wanted to open an old project from Finder without first deciding what to take off my Mac.

a video editor might only need a few frames from the middle of a film. why download the rest? that’s the idea behind Lumen: keep the film in the bucket. read the frames you need.

i wanted the choice of bucket to be mine, too. the drive encrypts files before they leave my machine. i can bring my own storage and mount it on macOS, Windows, or Linux.

there’s a way in for agents as well. they can search the drive or read part of a file over MCP. no clicking around in Finder.

it’s still a beta. if a project doesn’t open the way you expect, tell me where it gets stuck.

questions about lumen.

what is lumen?

an encrypted drive for files you don’t want to keep on your computer. open it in Finder or Explorer, or connect an agent to the same files through MCP.

does it work on my mac?

the universal download runs on Intel and Apple silicon Macs with macOS 12 Monterey or newer. install macFUSE before mounting your first drive. the setup guide walks you through it.

windows and linux?

yes. on Windows, install WinFsp and use the x64 ZIP. on Linux, build the FUSE mount from source. both use the same drive format as the Mac version.

how is this different from icloud, dropbox or google drive?

those services offer different ways to keep local or online-only files. Lumen mounts an S3-compatible bucket and reads the chunks an app requests, without having to mirror the whole drive.

how is it different from helumi?

Helumi’s desktop client is for macOS 26.2 and later and uses Cloudflare R2. Lumen also supports older Macs, Windows and Linux, lets you choose a bucket, and includes a local MCP server.

will it work with davinci, final cut, premiere, photoshop?

these apps see a mounted drive, so you can open files from their usual file picker. the beta tests include video scrubbing, but they don’t cover every app, codec or project.

where are my files stored?

in managed storage or in your own S3-compatible bucket. Lumen works with R2, B2, Storj and MinIO. either way, the bucket receives encrypted chunks, not readable files.

what if i lose my key?

keep a backup of your key. without the key or a recovery copy, neither Lumen nor your storage provider can decrypt the files for you.

does it fill up my disk?

mounting the drive doesn’t download its contents. the local index, cached data and files you keep offline still use space.

can i work offline?

you can search the local file index and read data that’s already cached or pinned. fetching a chunk that isn’t on your computer needs a connection.

what happens if i stop paying?

canceling doesn’t delete your files. the beta keeps mounts and reads available for 30 days. writes resume when you reactivate. if you use your own bucket, you still manage that account separately.

refunds?

you can request a full refund within three days of your first payment, once per person. renewals aren’t refundable. you can cancel at any time.

what does beta mean here?

the drive, encryption and billing have automated tests. installers and workflows are still changing. the changelog shows what’s shipped.
